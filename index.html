Task Procedures & Workflow Guide

Dedicated support that treats your business like my own
Client Journey — Full Process
From initial questionnaire through to post-settlement.
1
Step 1
Client Questionnaire
  • BA provides client name and email
  • Prepare and send the Client Questionnaire
  • Confirm if Cash Flow Calculator is required
  • Copy BA on all outgoing emails
2
Step 2
Review Zoho Form Submission
✓ Completed
  • Send thank-you letter
  • Proceed to Step 3
✗ Not Completed
  • Notify BA immediately
  • Await instructions
3
Step 3
BA Touch Base with Client
Meeting between BA and client to explain services and support the property search journey.
4
Step 4
Contract — Buyer's Engagement Form
  • Go to Zoho Sign
  • Prepare contract accurately
  • Review all details before sending
  • Send to client for signature
5
Step 5
Offer Details & WhatsApp Group
  • Send thank-you + Details for Offers document
  • Create WhatsApp Group: Internal Group — [Client Name]
6
Step 6
Suburb Shortlisting & Cold Calling
  • Shortlist suburbs based on client criteria
  • Begin cold calling for off-market properties
  • See Calling Agents tab for full process
7
Step 7
Offer Preparation
  • Use Offer Details confirmed by client
  • Request offer details from BA via WhatsApp group
  • Send screenshot for BA confirmation before submitting
8
Step 8
Offer Accepted — Receive Contract
  • Download/forward contract to client and Conveyancer
  • Copy BA on all emails
9
Step 9
Email Conveyancer
  • Request important contract dates
  • Copy client and BA
10
Step 10
Calendar Reminders
  • Set reminders 2–3 days before each date
  • Add BA as calendar invitee
11
Step 11
Update WhatsApp Group
  • Input confirmed dates in group description
  • Keep property details up to date
12
Step 12
Building & Pest Inspection
  • Organise inspection, confirm company with BA
  • Copy BA on all correspondence
13
Step 13
Request Rental Appraisal
  • Email PM for rental appraisal
  • Confirm PM choice with BA
14
Step 14
Forward Rental Appraisal
  • Forward to client and mortgage broker
  • Copy BA
15
Step 15
Pre-Settlement Inspection
  • Coordinate with sales agent and PM
  • Schedule 1 week to 2 days before settlement
16
Step 16
Settlement Reminder
  • Send reminder in WhatsApp group 2 days before settlement
17
Step 17 · Final
Close WhatsApp Group
  • Remove BA and WhatsApp Business account
  • Delete the group chat
End of Client Journey

Step 1
Research Agents
  • Go to Domain.com.au or RealEstate.com.au
  • Apply Buy or Sold filter for target suburb
  • Extract agent name and phone number
  • Save in Zoho CRM
2
Step 2
Prepare to Call
  • Open VoIPCloud — confirm headset and connection
  • Review agent details and keep script ready
3
Step 3
Make the Call
Follow script. Introduce yourself and ask about off-market or upcoming listings.

Step 4A — Live
Handle Response
✓ Interested
  • Ask pre-qualifying questions
  • Request walkthrough video via WhatsApp
✗ Not Interested
  • Thank them politely
  • Request to follow up later

Step 4B — Voicemail
Voicemail Protocol
  • Leave professional voicemail
  • Follow up call after 1 hour
  • If no answer — send Email or SMS

Step 5
